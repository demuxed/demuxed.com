Alright, gang, this one’s been a long time coming. Just to make sure we don’t bury the lede here: the traditional Demuxed conference is not happening in 2026. We’ll go into more details below, but instead we’re working with the MoQ Alliance to put together a scrappy, technology-focused event in October. It kinda named itself: **Demoqed**.

## Why

Hopefully at least some of you are curious as to why…and if you aren’t, pretend you are so we feel better.

The big one is… well the AI hype train apparently has a stop at every venue of our size in the Bay. To be fair, we’re at a weird size where our options are fairly limited to begin with, but the options for a Demuxed 26 home were *bleak* this year.

The venue frustrations just got us thinking more about the event this year and what we wanted to do. We kept coming back to the feeling of 2015, when we were a small gaggle of nerds in a room together talking about the edges of technology. AOM was born in 2015! People were still switching to HTML5! The rest of “the industry” seemed kinda dismissive of the web and open standards, but we just thought it was cool, so we got together without The Suits to talk about it.

12 years in, we decided to take a beat and try and chase that 2015 vibe this year. We’re going to take a step back from the mainstream Demuxed to help support a technology we’re excited about, and think about how we want Demuxed to serve the community next year and beyond.

## Demoqed and what’s happening in October

Demuxed Foundation is a part of the MoQ Alliance. Phil and I are personally excited about MoQ, and we’re going to help the alliance organize a half-day event on October 8th in San Francisco at the Alamo Drafthouse. To be clear, it’s not Demuxed-but-smaller, we want it to be its own dorky self. If you’re excited about and building MoQ stuff, we’d love to see you there. 

There’s also a *lot* going on that week. Our friends at Apple are planning another iteration of the HLS Interest Group, and we’re hoping to help organize another FOMS. That week in SF is also [Tech Week](https://www.tech-week.com/sf), so there will be hundreds of events, meetups, round tables, and, I’m quite sure, as much AI Thought Leadership as you could ever dream of.

The (barebones) website, and registration page for DEMOQED is up now, and if you've got an awesome MoQ talk idea to submit, there's a link right there - check it out 👇🏻

## Video stuff on the world wide web

🐰 The MediaBunny legend is back with [TurboRes](https://github.com/Vanilagy/turbores). Zig, Typescript, very fast ProRes decoding in the browser (or other JS environments), no hardware acceleration required. Side note, we were all shocked to discover at Demuxed 25 that Vanilagy is not, in fact, an anime character in real life despite the pfp. 

🏎️ It takes a special kind of writer to make you laugh out loud while reading about...media-aware network protocols. [Kixelated manages to do it every time](https://moq.dev/blog/cars2/), though. This time it mostly focuses on why he’s got a Lightning McQueen tattoo on his lower back (true story).

👐 SMPTE opened up [free access to their standards](https://www.smpte.org/blog/smpte-makes-its-standards-freely-accessible-openingstandards-library-to-the-global-media-technology-community).
